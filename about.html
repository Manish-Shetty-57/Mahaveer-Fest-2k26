About MAHAVEERA FEST 2K26

MAHAVEERA FEST 2K26 is the flagship intercollegiate mega-fest exclusively conceptualized for Pre-University (PU) students across Karnataka. Scheduled for 30 October 2026, it brings together students from 1st PUC and 2nd PUC to discover their potential and compete across Science, Commerce, Information Technology, and Humanities streams.
