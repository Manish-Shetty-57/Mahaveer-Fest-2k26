About the Fest

A Celebration of Excellence

MAHAVEERA FEST 2K26 is an intercollegiate extravaganza organized by the Internal Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC), Science, Commerce, IT, and Humanities Associations of Sri Mahaveera College, Moodbidri.

Designed to provide a competitive yet inclusive platform for Pre-University students across Karnataka, this flagship event brings together the brightest minds in Science, Commerce, Information Technology, and Humanities — all under one roof, in the spirit of healthy competition and academic camaraderie.

Whether you are a logical thinker, a creative artist, a tech wizard, or a business strategist, MAHAVEERA FEST 2K26 has an event crafted for you. Join us on 30th October 2026 for a day of unforgettable experiences, challenges, and celebration.

Important Information

Rules & Guidelines

General Rules

No registration fee — completely free to participate
Maximum 25 unique students per college across all events
Only one team per college per event is allowed
College ID card is mandatory for every participant
Authorization letter from Principal/HOD is mandatory
Judges' decision shall be final and binding
Participation certificates for all students

Day Logistics

Report before 8:45 AM on 30 October 2026
Complimentary breakfast & lunch provided for all
No TA/DA will be provided
Online registration closed • Spot registration is available at venue
Participation certificates issued to all registered participants
Venue: Sri Mahaveera College, Kodangallu - 574197, Moodbidri, D.K. District, Karnataka

Important Notice

A same student may participate in multiple events but will only be counted once towards the 25-student college quota.
